闪念Spark 隐私政策

更新日期：2026年9月18日

闪念Spark（以下简称“本应用”）是一款个人闪念记录工具。我们非常重视你的隐私，本政策说明本应用如何处理你的数据。

我们不收集什么

  • 不要求注册、登录，不收集任何账号信息
  • 不使用任何分析、统计或广告追踪工具
  • 不与任何第三方共享、出售你的数据（下方“AI 转写润饰”这一项可选功能除外，且需你主动开启、主动确认）
  • 不包含任何第三方软件开发工具包（SDK）

你的笔记和录音存在哪里

你在本应用中记录的文字和语音，只保存在你自己的 iPhone 本地存储中，不会上传到我们的任何服务器——因为我们没有服务器。

语音转写

语音转文字功能完全在你的设备本地完成（使用 iOS 系统自带的语音识别能力），录音内容不会离开你的设备。

可选功能：导出到 Obsidian

如果你主动选择连接一个文件夹（例如 iCloud 云盘中的 Obsidian 笔记库），本应用会把你的笔记内容以 Markdown 文件形式写入该文件夹。这些文件的后续同步由你自己的 iCloud（或其他你选择的同步服务）负责，与本应用无关，本应用不参与、不经手这个同步过程。

可选功能：AI 转写润饰（Pro）

这是本应用唯一会把你的内容发送给第三方的功能，默认关闭，需要你在设置里主动完成以下步骤才会启动：

  1. 你自己选择一家第三方 AI 服务商（例如 DeepSeek、OpenAI 兼容接口，或你自己搭建的服务），并填入其服务地址、模型名和你自己的 API 密钥；
  2. 应用会明确弹窗告知这些文字将被发送到哪一个地址，你必须主动点击确认才会真正开启——填完信息本身不会自动启用；
  3. 如果你之后把服务地址换成另一家，应用会视为一次新的第三方，重新弹窗要求你再次确认。

开启后，只有语音转写出来的文字会被发送给你自己选定的这家第三方服务商，用于把口语顺成书面语（加标点、改错字、去口水词）。原始录音音频永远不会离开你的设备，也不会经过我们或任何第三方。

密钥保存在你设备的钥匙串中，我们和任何第三方都无法获取。你可以随时在设置中关闭此功能或清除密钥，关闭后转写只在设备本地完成。

数据删除

删除本应用即会清除你设备上的所有本地数据。你也可以随时在应用内删除单条笔记。

Spark Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Spark (“the App”) is a personal, fleeting-thought capture tool. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

What we do not collect

  • No account, no sign-up, no sign-in — we don't collect any account information
  • No analytics, statistics, or advertising trackers of any kind
  • We do not share or sell your data to any third party (with the single exception described under “Optional Feature: AI Transcript Polishing” below, which only runs if you deliberately turn it on and confirm it)
  • No third-party SDKs are included in the App

Where your notes and recordings live

The text and voice recordings you create in the App are stored only in your iPhone's local storage. Nothing is uploaded to any server we operate — because we don't operate any server.

Speech transcription

Speech-to-text runs entirely on your device (using Apple's built-in on-device speech recognition). Your recordings never leave your device for transcription.

Optional Feature: Export to Obsidian

If you choose to connect a folder (for example, an Obsidian vault stored in iCloud Drive), the App writes your notes as Markdown files into that folder. Any further syncing of those files is handled by your own iCloud (or whichever sync service you chose) — the App is not involved in, and has no visibility into, that syncing process.

Optional Feature: AI Transcript Polishing (Pro)

This is the only feature in the App that sends any of your content to a third party. It is off by default, and turning it on requires you to complete these steps yourself:

  1. You choose a third-party AI provider (for example DeepSeek, an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, or a service you host yourself) and enter its endpoint URL, model name, and your own API key;
  2. The App shows a confirmation dialog stating exactly which address your text will be sent to, and you must actively tap to confirm — simply filling in the fields does not enable the feature by itself;
  3. If you later change the endpoint to a different provider, the App treats this as a new third party and asks you to confirm again.

Once enabled, only the transcribed text is sent to the third-party provider you configured, in order to turn spoken language into cleaner written text (adding punctuation, fixing misheard words, removing filler words). Your original audio recording never leaves your device, and is never sent to us or to any third party.

Your API key is stored in your device's Keychain; neither we nor any third party can access it. You can disable this feature or clear your key at any time in Settings, after which transcription happens entirely on-device again.

Data deletion

Deleting the App removes all local data on your device. You can also delete individual notes at any time within the App.
